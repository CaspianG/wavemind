# Quantum hypothesis-selection gate: rules frozen before source selection

Prepared 2026-09-07T15:09:55Z, before this gate's new literature search.
Parent research state: `1558f08`. This is selection only, not an experiment,

## Fixed scope and search budget

Inspect at most three precise unresolved problems in quantum error correction
or fault tolerance, using primary papers and author-maintained software/data.
Prefer publications/revisions from 2025 through 2026-09-07, with older exact
baselines where necessary. Exclude later-dated results and distinguish an
author's open question from an application gap inferred in this audit.
At most 12 targeted search queries; stop expanding a candidate once a fatal
no-go is established. Read relevant methods/results/limitations, not abstracts
alone. Unknown novelty or inaccessible essential evidence counts as no-go.

## All six admission requirements must pass

1. **Exact task:** fixed input, allowed information, output and operational
   setting; a precise unresolved question supported by a primary source.
2. **Novel mechanism:** a minimal new falsifiable claim with a specific
   mechanism or mathematical invariant, contrasted with the strongest
   directly relevant method found. Merely combining known decoders, adapting
   weights, scheduling fallbacks, or naming a useful objective does not pass.
   A missing comparison is not evidence of novelty.
3. **Reproducibility:** public simulator/data and baseline source with
   identifiable versions and documented access/license status. No essential
   unavailable hardware, proprietary traces, paid endpoint or human pilot.
   A public landing page alone is insufficient. No new download or execution
   is required for this selection gate.
4. **Cheap lethal test:** a counterexample/proof obstruction or small controlled
   experiment executable in <=2 CPU-hours, <=8 GB RAM, <=100,000 shots per
   candidate, with matched data and costs. A kill test can reject but cannot
   establish rare-event, asymptotic or fault-tolerance performance. If this
   budget cannot falsify the stated claim, reject or leave it unselected.
5. **Direct operational value:** aim at >=2x reduction in total decoder
   latency/compute at non-inferior logical error, or >=20% logical-error
   reduction at non-increased total decoder cost, or a precisely specified
   capability previously unavailable at the same resource bound. Include
   preprocessing/training/calibration/fallback costs, not just one kernel.
   A later performance protocol must freeze 95% uncertainty intervals,
   dependence handling and noninferiority margins before scoring. No utility
   inference from a quantum-inspired analogy.
6. **Clean separation from prior results:** no R8 rerun, reinterpretation of
   old passes, hardware superiority claim from simulator data, or product
   transfer without measured workflow evidence. Freeze any selected claim,
   negative controls and complete experiment protocol before implementation.

## Deterministic decision rule

Record each gate as pass, fail, or unknown with evidence. Fail/unknown in any
gate means no-go for implementation. If more than one candidate passes all
six, choose lowest estimated falsifier cost, then fewest unverified external
assumptions, then oldest primary-source publication date. If none passes,
select none and do not spend resources on implementation. Do not change these
criteria after reading candidate outcomes.
